About the city

Omniscience is a solarpunk metropolis at golden hour that never quite ends. Towers avoid the right angle. Materials are gold, bronze, glass, and leaf. Elevated rings carry people the way a bloodstream carries heat. At street level: parks, white geodesic biomes, and the sense that the city was grown as much as it was drawn.

The Spire is the landmark and the metaphor — organic ribs, vertical glass, a twisting climb. If the film has a face, this is it. Not a villain’s tower. A listening post for a species that finally learned to hear itself.
