The Studio

Sean Behm Founder, Source Sculptures

We’re a studio driven by sculpture, story, and the built world. From monumental public work to fully imagined cinematic cities, we make futures that don’t just look possible — they feel inhabited.

Omniscience asks what happens when a civilization learns to listen to itself. Not surveillance. Belonging. Knowledge as a shared atmosphere, the way light already is.

The Vision

Omniscience is not a camera on every corner. It is the moment a culture can feel the whole the way a body feels a fever — instantly, without cruelty. The film treats awareness as architecture: a spire that listens, rings that carry more than people, gardens that remember.

Identity, Myth, The Source, Unity

Towers twist like living ribs. Transit loops are circular, never extractive. Geodesic biomes sit in the streets the way lungs sit in a chest. This is solarpunk without slogan: a place you could walk, and want to stay.

The Spire, The Rings, The Domes

Hopeful science fiction is not naivete. It is a design brief. Omniscience pictures energy, transit, and attention as circular systems — the same ethic Source Sculptures brings to physical sculpture: presence, reflection, a pulse you can feel in the chest.

From Source Spirits in Amsterdam to this skyline, the studio’s work keeps asking the same question: can making — metal, film, light — teach us how to belong? Omniscience is that question at city scale.

Omniscience helps a future stand out with bold design and a mind that still has a heart. We mix sculpture, story, and the city.
